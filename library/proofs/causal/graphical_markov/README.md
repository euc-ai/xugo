# graphical_markov — structural σ-algebra families and the global Markov property

Graph theory states separation and probability states conditional
independence; the one object that connects them is a structural
σ-algebra family on a DAG: two families of sub-σ-algebras indexed by
the nodes, the information G(j) and the noise N(j) of each node, with
the noise family independent and each G(j) contained in the σ-algebra
its parents' information and its own noise generate
(`StructuralSigmaFamilyDef`). Separation in the DAG then gives
conditional independence of the joined information σ-algebras, for any
state spaces, with no kernels and no pointwise integration of
mechanisms. The route is the local Markov property, proved by
induction along the graph, and the semigraphoid local-to-global theorem
of `relations/graph/d_separation`, into which the σ-algebra laws of
`probability/conditional_independence` are packed here.

The bridge has two instances: a legal model with independent noise, on
its own graph — which covers every interventional distribution, since
an intervened model is a model — and the regime family of a model and
an intervention, on the graph with a regime root added.

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `StructuralSigmaFamilyDef` | `structural_sigma_family(Ω, S, μ, V, E, G, N)`: `dag_on(V, E)`, N an independent family on V, each G(j) a σ-algebra inside the one generated by its parents' G and N(j) |
| `SigmaFamilySemigraphoid` | the joins of a family of sub-σ-algebras form a semigraphoid under conditional independence — card |
| `StructuralFamilyLocalMarkov` | each node's information is independent of its non-descendants' given its parents' — card |
| `StructuralFamilyGlobalMarkov` | separation of disjoint groups gives conditional independence of their joins — card |
| `SCMStructuralFamily` | a legal model with independent noise is a structural family on its own graph — card |
| `SCMGlobalMarkov` | in a legal model with independent noise, separation gives V_X ⊥ V_Y \| V_Z: Rule 1's independence form — card |
| `RegimeStructuralFamily` | the regime family of do(Z = z) is a structural family on the graph with a root above Z — card |
