# dirac_index — the twisted Spin Dirac operator and its index

A finite Spin atlas and its projected spinor realization determine the induced metric connection. The operator is the sum c(ej) times the covariant derivative in ej, twisted by a projected complex bundle connection. Its principal symbol is i c(xi sharp), from the even spinors to odd spinors. Definitions use derivatives, the local orthonormal frames and the explicit Clifford matrices.

The A-hat class is the constant-one formal square root of the Todd class of the complexified tangent bundle; the root identity identifies it with the usual product $\frac{x/2}{\sinh(x/2)}$. The symbol-character comparison and the general elliptic index formula give the twisted Spin formula. Spin-c and arbitrary Clifford modules are distinct extensions and no formula for them is asserted here.
