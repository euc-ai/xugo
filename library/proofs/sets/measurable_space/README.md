# measurable_space — measurable maps between explicit carriers

Measurable maps preserve measurable sets by relative inverse image. Identity, composition and a generated-family criterion give one mapping interface for arbitrary measurable spaces. The σ-algebra a map generates by pullback, the one a family of coordinates generates, and the product σ-algebras of an indexed family and of a pair are defined here, with the criteria that read measurability into a product coordinate by coordinate. A finite product of finite discrete spaces is discrete, and a map out of a product with a finite discrete factor is measurable when each of its sections is.
