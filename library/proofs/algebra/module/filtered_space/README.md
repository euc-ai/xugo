# filtered_space — bases adapted to finite filtrations whose steps have dimension at most one

A finite increasing filtration with increments at most one admits a basis indexed by its growing layers. The result concerns vector spaces only; geometric applications supply their own filtration and order laws.

## Members

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `FilteredSpaceAdaptedBasis` | a finite filtration with dimension increases at most one has a finite basis with distinct entry layers whose initial selections span every filtration piece |
