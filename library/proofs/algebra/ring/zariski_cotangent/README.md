# zariski_cotangent — the Zariski cotangent space m/m² at a point with finite residue field, bounded by the Jacobian

At a point of a ring generated by finitely many elements of a field, with finite residue field, the gradients of the relations among the generators bound the cotangent space m/m² from above. Each member of the ring expands to first order around its image under the residue field's Frobenius (partial_derivative.TaylorModuloSquare), which reads m/m² as the coefficient vectors of the expansions modulo those of the relations; a rank count over the residue field then makes any two members dependent modulo m², and a maximal ideal with that property is spanned modulo m² by one member.

## Members

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `PairDependentModuloRelations` | relation vectors among `e(i) ∈ ker φ` whose M-span has dimension at least n ⇒ any two coefficient families are dependent modulo `I` |
| `CotangentLine` | a maximal ideal whose members are pairwise dependent modulo m² is `u·R + m²` for one nonzero `u` |
| `JacobianCotangentBound` | `S = k[t] ⊆ M`, `k` finite, a point φ with kernel m ≠ 0 and finite residue field: gradients of the relations whose M-span has dimension at least n ⇒ m/m² is spanned by one nonzero `u` |
