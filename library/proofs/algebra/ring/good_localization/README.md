# good_localization — fixed denominator certificates and finite algebra models

Polynomial Bezout identities can be cleared to subring coefficients with one nonzero denominator. Two finite algebra models of the same fraction field agree after one fixed localization: each spans the field over the fractions of the scalars, and finite-module localization supplies a common denominator.

## Members

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `SeparablePolynomialGoodOpen` | A separable polynomial F over S admits U F + W F′ = a with U, W over S and nonzero a ∈ S, when L is the fraction field of S |
| `FiniteModelsAgreeAfterLocalization` | Two subring models B, C, finite as S-modules with common fraction field L, agree after inverting one nonzero a ∈ S |
| `ModelsAgreeInSubfield` | Two finite models with fraction field a subfield K agree after inverting one nonzero scalar, read in the ambient field |
