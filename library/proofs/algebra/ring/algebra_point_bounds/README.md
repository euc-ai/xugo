# algebra_point_bounds — how many points an algebra has

The points of `algebra_points`, counted. Points fixing the coefficient field of an algebra are characters of the algebra with values in the field, so they are independent in the linear dual (`linear_independence.CharactersIndependent`), and a finite-dimensional algebra has at most its dimension many points. For a general prescription the chapter counts points under the operations that build rings: a ring generated by a finite set has finitely many points in a finite ring, and inverting d keeps exactly the points that do not kill d (`AlgebraPointsAway`). Inside a field the same localization reads as a restriction bijection onto the points not killing d, and the points over one value of a coordinate, or killing the value of a nonzero polynomial, are bounded by the degree of a monic relation (`PointsOverValueBound`, `KilledPointsBound`). The points of S[G] agreeing on S are boundedly many for G finite and integral (`AgreeingPointsBound`), and a ring finite over k[t] has boundedly many points over each value of t (`UniformFibreBound`).

## Members

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `AlgebraModuleOn` | A ring map from a field makes a commutative ring a module over the field |
| `AlgebraPointsIndependent` | Normalized algebra maps fixing the coefficient field are independent in the dual |
| `AlgebraPointsBound` | A finite-dimensional algebra has finitely many normalized points, at most its dimension |
| `AlgebraPointsGeneratedFinite` | A ring generated over S by a finite set has finitely many points in a finite ring |
| `AlgebraPointsAway` | When R is S with d inverted, restriction to S is a bijection from the points of R onto the points of S at which d is a unit |
| `AlgebraPointsAwayCardLe` | Inverting a member d of S with an inverse e in the ambient ring adds no points: S[1/d] has at most as many points as S, when S has finitely many |
| `AlgebraPointsAwayInField` | Inside a field, inverting a nonzero element loses exactly the points that kill it |
| `PointsOverRelationBound` | Points of S[z] agreeing on S are at most deg g, for z a root of a monic g over S |
| `PointsOverValueBound` | Points of k[t][z] over one value of t are at most the degree of a monic relation of z |
| `KilledPointsBound` | Points killing the value at t of a nonzero polynomial u are boundedly many over its roots |
| `AlgebraPointsAwayInFieldRestriction` | Inside a field, restriction to S is a bijection from the points of S[1/d] onto the points of S not killing d |
| `AgreeingPointsBound` | Points of S[G] fixing k ⊆ S, G finite and integral over S, that agree on S are boundedly many, one bound for every subfield of values |
| `UniformFibreBound` | A subring holding k and t, finite over k[t], has boundedly many points over each value of t, one bound for every subfield of values |
