# homogeneous_polynomial — forms: the homogeneous polynomials of one degree over a commutative ring

A form of degree d in the variables up to n over a ring R is a polynomial each of whose monomials has degree d. This chapter holds the definition, with its two parts stated as laws that proofs cite in place of the unfolded definition; the forms of one degree and their closure under sums, negatives, products and powers; the part of a polynomial of one degree and how multiplying by a form moves the parts, and the truncation at a degree; carrying a form's coefficients along a ring homomorphism; how the value of a form scales with the point, and the ratios and numerators that follow; partial derivatives, Euler's identity and homogenization; evaluation at a point of a field as a ring homomorphism; and the gradient of a polynomial at a point. A polynomial of degree at most B lies in the ideal finitely many forms generate, the set of their combinations (submodule.SpanFamilyCombinations), with cofactors of degree at most B. Over an infinite integral domain that membership is read pointwise, and the ideal is prime exactly when degree-bounded pointwise conditions hold — the reading the elementarity step can carry between algebraically closed fields. The projective curves (projective_curve and the chapters built on it) read their equations as forms from here.

## Ideals generated by forms

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `PolynomialBoundUpTo` | a polynomial has degree at most d in the n + 1 variables below n + 1 exactly when it is a polynomial in the variables up to n whose monomials where it is nonzero have degree at most d |
| `BoundedIdealMembership` | a polynomial of degree at most B lies in the ideal finitely many forms generate exactly when it is a combination of them with cofactors of degree at most B |
| `PointwiseIdealMembership` | over an infinite integral domain, a polynomial of degree at most B lies in the ideal finitely many forms generate exactly when at every point it is Σ P(j)(x)·H(j)(x) for cofactors of degree at most B |
| `FormIdealPrimeCriterion` | over an infinite integral domain, the ideal finitely many forms generate is prime exactly when 1 is no pointwise combination with constant cofactors and, at every degree bound D, a product of polynomials of degree at most D that is a pointwise combination at degree 2D has a factor that is one at degree D |
