# kaehler — derivations of an algebra into a module, the module of Kähler differentials and its universal property

An algebra over a ring is a ring `S` with a ring map `f` from the base
ring `R`. A derivation of it into a module `N` over `S` is an additive
map `D` from `S`'s carrier into `N`'s with the Leibniz rule
`D(uv) = u • D(v) + v • D(u)` that vanishes on the constants `f(a)`:
`derivation_on(R, S, f, N, D)`, minted beside the partial derivatives in
`partial_derivative`, whose derivatives of the free algebra are the first
example of one. The module of Kähler differentials $\Omega_{S/R}$ is the target
of the universal derivation, the one every other derivation factors
through.

It is built from the free module over `S` on the members of `S`'s carrier,
`free_module_record(S, S.carrier)` with generators δ_u, modulo the
submodule its relations span: $\delta_{u+v} - (\delta_u + \delta_v)$,
$\delta_{uv} - (u • \delta_v + v • \delta_u)$ and $\delta_{f(a)}$. It is the corpus's quotient
module, so its vectors are cosets of finitely supported functions on `S`'s
carrier, and it is one module record over `S` itself,
`kaehler_module(R, S, f) : module_ops(B, set(B → B))`. The universal
derivation `universal_derivation(R, S, f)` takes `u` to the coset of δ_u,
written du. The relations lie in the free module only when every `f(a)`
lies in `S`'s carrier, so every statement asks `f` to be a ring map.

The universal property is stated in three parts: a homomorphism out of
$\Omega_{S/R}$ composed with d is a derivation, every derivation is such a
composite, and the homomorphism is unique — agreement on the du suffices,
since the du span the module. Together they make `h ↦ h ∘ d` a bijection
from the homomorphisms out of $\Omega_{S/R}$ onto the derivations. Two
computations follow. The differentials of the free algebra on a set of
variables `X` are the free module on `X` over the algebra, du going to its
partial derivatives (∂_x u)_x. For a tower `R → S → T` along `f` and `g`,
the first fundamental exact sequence
$\Omega_{S/R} \otimes_S T \to \Omega_{T/R} \to \Omega_{T/S} \to 0$ is exact, the tensor product
taken over `S` with `T` a module through `g`.

The comparison of $\Omega_{S/R}$ with the degree-zero homology of the cotangent
complex is stated in `algebra/cotangent/cotangent_complex`, which stands
on this chapter: a ring chapter does not import the cotangent domain.

| member | statement |
|---|---|
| `KaehlerRelationsDef` | `kaehler_relations(R, S, f)`: the span in the free module over `S` on `S`'s carrier of $\delta_{u+v} - (\delta_u + \delta_v)$, $\delta_{uv} - (u • \delta_v + v • \delta_u)$ and $\delta_{f(a)}$ |
| `KaehlerModuleDef` | `kaehler_module(R, S, f)` = `quotient_module(free_module_record(S, S.carrier), kaehler_relations(R, S, f))`, $\Omega_{S/R}$ |

| `KaehlerRelationsSubmodule` | for `f` a ring map, the relations are a submodule of the free module — card |
| `KaehlerModuleOn` | $\Omega_{S/R}$ is a module over `S` — card |

## The universal property

| member | statement |
|---|---|
| `DerivationComposeHomomorphism` | a derivation followed by a homomorphism of modules over `S` is a derivation — card |
| `KaehlerUniversalExists` | every derivation `D` into a module `N` over `S` is `h ∘ d` on `S`'s carrier for a homomorphism $h : \Omega_{S/R} \to N$ — card |
| `KaehlerUniversalUnique` | two homomorphisms out of $\Omega_{S/R}$ agreeing on every du agree on $\Omega_{S/R}$ — card |

## Monic evaluation presentations

For an exact monic presentation B=S[y]/(F), the differential module is B/(F′(y)), generated by dy. The derivative is allowed to vanish; the presentation supplies the sole relation.

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `MonicEvaluationPresentation` | The relative differential module of an exact monic presentation is the derivative quotient, generated by dy. — card |
