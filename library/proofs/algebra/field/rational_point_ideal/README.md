# rational_point_ideal — the ideal of a rational point of a plane chart, and when it is invertible

Inside a field M over a subfield l, let s and y be elements of M and ψ a ring map from l[s][y] to l fixing l: a rational point of the chart l[s][y], monogenic over l[s]. The point takes s and y into l (`MonogenicChartSubring`) and kills x − ψ(x) for every x (`PointKillsDifference`), and the elements congruent to their values modulo an ideal form a subring holding l (`PointResidueSubring`), so the kernel of ψ is generated by s − ψ(s) and y − ψ(y) (`MonogenicPointIdeal`). When y is a root of G over l[s], the two generators are related by (y − ψ(y))·g = (s − ψ(s))·h with ψ(g) = ψ(G′(y)) (`SimpleRootRelation`), and for s ∉ l a relation with ψ(g) ≠ 0 makes the kernel an invertible fractional ideal of l[s][y] (`MonogenicRelationInvertible`). At a simple root this is the Jacobian criterion of `invertible_fractional_ideal.JacobianRelationInvertible`, read on the chart.

## Members

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `PointResidueSubring` | The elements congruent to their values modulo an ideal form a subring holding l |
| `PointKillsDifference` | A ring map from a subring T to a part l of T that it fixes kills x − ψ(x) for every x in T |
| `MonogenicChartSubring` | For s, y in a field over a subfield l, l[s] and l[s][y] are subrings holding l, s and y, and an l-point ψ of l[s][y] takes s and y into l |
| `MonogenicPointIdeal` | The kernel of an l-point ψ of l[s][y], monogenic over l[s], is generated by s − ψ(s) and y − ψ(y) |
| `SimpleRootRelation` | A root y of G over l[s] relates y − ψ(y) and s − ψ(s) through an element with value ψ(G′(y)) |
| `MonogenicRelationInvertible` | A relation (y − ψ(y))·g = (s − ψ(s))·h with ψ(g) ≠ 0 makes the kernel of an l-point of l[s][y], monogenic over l[s], an invertible fractional ideal |
