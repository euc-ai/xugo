# polynomial_brouwer — polynomial Brouwer fixed points over arbitrary real closed fields

The real topological Brouwer theorem supplies the source, `real_polynomial_geometry.BrouwerSource`, stated on the property `polynomial_brouwer_property` of `semialgebraic_properties`. At a common degree bound of the coordinate polynomials the property quantifies all coefficients and permits any finite dimension; elementarity carries it from the reals, and it is read at the map (`ByElementarity(…).At(P)`).

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `PolynomialBrouwer` | Every polynomial self-map of a finite-dimensional cube over a real closed field has a fixed point. |
