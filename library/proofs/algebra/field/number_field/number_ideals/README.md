# number_ideals — ideal factorization in rings of integers

In the ring of integers every nonzero ideal has a finite quotient and the ring is integrally closed. From these two facts the ideal-factorization chapter's general argument gives each nonzero ideal a finite height, makes it invertible, and factors it uniquely into prime ideals.

## Members

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `NumberRingFactorization` | Every nonzero ideal in a ring of integers is invertible and has a unique finite prime ideal exponent factorization |
| `NumberFractionalIdeals` | Fractional ideals of a number ring are invertible exactly when they are bounded |
