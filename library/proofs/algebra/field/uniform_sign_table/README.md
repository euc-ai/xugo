# uniform_sign_table — uniform sign tables for every finite family over the parameter ring, and the realizable sign vectors they control

Every finite family of polynomials over the parameter ring has a uniform sign table under any condition, by induction on the degrees: the Cohen–Hörmander step of parametric_sign_table reduces a family whose first member has a fixed degree and leading sign to the family of the other members, the derivative and the pseudo-remainders, whose members from the derivative on have lower degree. The step is reached by three reductions that keep the table: under a condition that fixes every coefficient's sign, the coefficients of sign zero are dropped, so that every member keeps its leading coefficient where it does not vanish; a member of top degree is moved to the front; and that member is lowered through the step (LowerTopDegreeMember), which leaves a family whose members of top degree all come before the member's index. `parameter_ring.go` holds the reductions and, after them, the induction (ParameterRingSignTable). `sign_vectors.go` reads it at a list of integer polynomials: one parameter-polynomial sign rule then determines the ordered tables in every real closed field on the host. Quantifier elimination over real closed fields reads the realizable sign vectors through RealizableSignControl; ParameterizedSignTable states the ordered tables those rows come from.

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `DerivativeRemainderFamily` | The family of the other members, the derivative and the pseudo-remainders over the parameter ring has members of lower degree from the derivative on, and its uniform table gives the family's. |
| `DropZeroSignCoefficients` | Under a condition that fixes the signs of a family's coefficients, dropping those of sign zero leaves parameter polynomials of no larger degree; a member other than the zero polynomial keeps the family's coefficient at its degree, of a nonzero sign, so its leading coefficient does not vanish where the member does not; and the trimmed family's uniform table gives the family's. |
| `LowerTopDegreeMember` | A family whose member at c has top degree and a fixed nonzero leading sign, its later members lower, gives through the Cohen–Hörmander family of that member moved to the front a family whose members of top degree all come before c, and whose uniform table gives the family's. |
| `MoveMemberToFront` | Exchanging a member with the first keeps the indices of the family, and the moved family's uniform table gives the family's. |
| `ParameterRingSignTable` | Every finite family of polynomials over the parameter ring has a uniform sign table, under any condition. |
| `ParameterizedSignTable` | One parameter-polynomial sign rule determines ordered tables in every real closed field on the host. |
| `RealizableSignControl` | Realizable sign vectors are determined uniformly by finitely many parameter signs. |
