# complex_polynomial — the fundamental theorem of algebra

Every nonconstant complex polynomial has a root. Its modulus grows at infinity, and the minimum of its squared modulus on a compact square, a polynomial expression in the two real parts, is therefore a global minimum. Translation and perturbation along the first nonconstant term rule out a nonzero minimum. The complex field is therefore algebraically closed, which is the statement the algebraic closure chapter reads.
