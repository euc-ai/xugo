# k_compact_support — compactly supported K classes represented by relative triples

A relative triple consists of two uniformly finite complex projection bundles and a continuous map between them which is invertible outside a compact set. Its equivalence relation is generated by homotopy with uniformly compact support, addition of degenerate triples, and direct sum. The resulting group is compactly supported K⁰.

Compact support is part of the representative and of a homotopy. Pullback is asserted for proper maps. Extension along an open inclusion uses a compact neighborhood of the noninvertible locus and glues by the specified isomorphism outside it; it is not entrywise zero extension of a projection.
