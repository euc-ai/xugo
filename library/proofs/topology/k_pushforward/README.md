# k_pushforward — compact-support pushforward through a specified complex tubular neighborhood

A complex tubular presentation consists of a projection bundle over the source and a homeomorphism from its total space onto an open neighborhood in the target, carrying the zero section to the specified embedding. The presentation includes the complex orientation; an arbitrary real tubular chart does not supply one.

Pushforward is the compact-support Thom map, followed by transport through the tubular homeomorphism and open extension. Independence of a geometric tubular choice is proved by its consumer from compatible germs and homotopies.
