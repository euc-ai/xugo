# k_theory — virtual differences of finite complex projection bundles

K⁰ is the Grothendieck group of finite projection bundles. A representative is an ordered pair (E,F), read as [E]−[F]. Two pairs are equivalent when E⊕F′ and E′⊕F become isomorphic after addition of the same bundle. Thus cancellation is not assumed for the bundle monoid.

Addition, multiplication and pullback descend to sets of equivalent representatives. Compact Hausdorff bases give homotopy invariance; the dimension difference at a point identifies its ring with the integers. The relative compact-support theory is a separate chapter.
