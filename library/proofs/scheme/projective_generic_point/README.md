# projective_generic_point — the generic point of a projective equation curve and its regular function field

The curve's generic point lives in M because M is algebraically closed and holds a transcendental, and its field is a regular function field over k: the Hilbert function's linear growth leaves one transcendental coordinate, and primality over M leaves no new algebraic constant.

## The generic point

| Member | Statement |
|---|---|
| `TranscendenceAtGenericPoint` | a point of a field read along a coefficient map from k, with the ideal as its vanishing forms and an eventually linear Hilbert function of positive slope, has one coordinate transcendental over k with the rest algebraic over it |
| `FormQuotients` | the field k(x) generated by the coordinates of a point with a coordinate one consists of quotients $\frac{F(x)}{G(x)}$ of forms over k of one degree, G(x) ≠ 0 |
| `HomogenizationInIdeal` | when the forms over k vanishing at x lie in the ideal of E, the homogenization of a polynomial f over k at forms F, G of one degree, with $\frac{F(x)}{G(x)}$ a root of f, lies in the ideal of E over M |
| `GenericFieldExactConstants` | primality over the algebraically closed M leaves no member of the generic field algebraic over k outside k |
| `PrimeIdealGenericPoint` | a prime ideal of the polynomials that misses Xⱼ has a generic point in the fraction field of the quotient, normalized at j, at which exactly its forms vanish |
| `CurveGenericPoint` | the function field of the curve over M holds a generic point over k with first nonzero coordinate one |
| `GenericPointIntoAmbient` | a point with one coordinate transcendental over k and the rest algebraic over it has an image in an algebraically closed M holding a transcendental, with the same vanishing polynomials |
| `AlgebraicPointIntoAmbient` | a point with every coordinate algebraic over k has an image in an algebraically closed M, with the same vanishing polynomials |
| `GenericFieldRegular` | the field of a generic point of the curve in an algebraically closed M is a regular function field over k |
| `GenericPointExists` | a smooth projective equation curve has a generic point in an algebraically closed field holding a transcendental, and its field is a regular function field over k |
